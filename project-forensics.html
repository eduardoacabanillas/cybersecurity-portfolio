Windows 11 Digital Forensics Investigation

An academic investigation of a suspected internal breach involving a Windows 11 workstation, focused on digital artifacts, evidence integrity, and reporting.

01 / Objective

The question behind the work.

Examine Windows 11 workstation evidence in a suspected-breach scenario using a structured forensic workflow, and document potential evidence and conclusions.

02 / Method

Explore the workflow.

Choose a step to see the investigative process. These descriptions reflect the documented project—not fabricated screenshots or logs.

STEP 01 — Review evidence

Define the available evidence and examine relevant data using Autopsy.

STEP 02 — Recover artifacts

Recover deleted files and review browser artifacts, system logs, and user activity.

STEP 03 — Identify items

Select and document potential evidence relevant to the investigation.

STEP 04 — Verify handling

Apply evidence-handling and hash-verification practices as part of the forensic process.

STEP 05 — Write report

Produce an investigation report covering methodology, evidence, findings, and conclusions.

03 / Result

What I completed.

  • Recovered deleted files and examined digital artifacts using Autopsy.
  • Identified and documented 10 potential pieces of forensic evidence.
  • Used evidence-handling and hash-verification practices.
  • Produced a technical investigation report.

04 / Reflection

What it demonstrates.

This case study demonstrates an organized approach to digital artifact examination, evidence documentation, and communicating conclusions without overstating what the evidence proves.

Phishing Incident Investigation & Response

A simulated phishing investigation conducted as part of my WGU cybersecurity capstone, using Elastic SIEM to review security alerts, evaluate evidence, and document a response.

01 / Objective

The question behind the work.

Investigate a simulated phishing incident, determine its likely root cause and scope from the available evidence, select appropriate remediation actions, and communicate findings clearly.

02 / Method

Explore the workflow.

Choose a step to see the investigative process. These descriptions reflect the documented project—not fabricated screenshots or logs.

STEP 01 — Review alerts

Examine the relevant security alerts and supporting logs in Elastic SIEM as an initial incident triage step.

STEP 02 — Correlate evidence

Gather and analyze available evidence to understand activity surrounding the simulated phishing event.

STEP 03 — Test hypotheses

Evaluate plausible explanations and determine the likely root cause and scope based on the evidence available.

STEP 04 — Validate response

Select and implement remediation actions within the simulation, then assess whether those actions were effective.

STEP 05 — Report findings

Document investigation findings, remaining risks, actions taken, and conclusions in executive and technical reports.

03 / Result

What I completed.

  • Reviewed and analyzed SIEM alerts and supporting evidence.
  • Determined a root-cause and scope assessment in the simulated case.
  • Selected and validated remediation actions within the scenario.
  • Produced an executive summary and technical incident report.

04 / Reflection

What it demonstrates.

This case study demonstrates an evidence-driven investigation workflow, decision-making under uncertainty, remediation validation, and the importance of writing clearly for both technical and executive audiences.
